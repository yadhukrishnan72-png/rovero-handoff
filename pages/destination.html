      Questions, answered

      Does the eSIM work across all of {the}?

      Yes, wherever there is local coverage and your phone supports eSIM.

      Does the eSIM work across the whole of {name}?

      It works in the countries listed for your plan, subject to local coverage and a supported device. Tap "countries" on a plan to see where it works.

      Why do plans cover different countries?

      Each package connects to a set of partner networks. Smaller plans cover fewer countries, and larger plans cover more. Check the list before you buy.

      When does my plan start?

      When the eSIM first connects to a supported network, or when the package's activation policy begins.

      Can I install it before I travel?

      Yes, and we recommend it. Install at home on Wi-Fi, then switch it on when you land.

      Will my regular SIM still work?

      On most phones, yes. Keep it active for calls and SMS while the eSIM handles data.

      Can I use hotspot?

      It depends on the package and your device settings, but many plans support tethering.

      Do I need Wi-Fi to install?

      Yes. You need an internet connection to download the eSIM profile.
